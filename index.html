            Connect your own Pollen

            Corner Office runs on Bring Your Own Pollen. Before anything happens, here is exactly what connecting means:

            1. You'll authorize on enter.pollinations.ai with a device code - nothing is spent by opening it.
            2. On the consent screen, tick the keys permission. It lets this app create your floor's agents on your own account (private prompt agents named corner-office-…). You can see and delete them any time in your Pollinations dashboard.
            3. Set a finite provider budget on the consent screen. It is not a guaranteed spending maximum: provider checks use estimates and a final charge may exceed the remaining budget. Every agent run is paid from your Pollen at standard model rates - this app takes no cut and never sees your balance beyond what you approve.
            4. Without keys, you can still explore: floors run through plain model calls on your Pollen, clearly marked, no agents are created.

            Running a workday sends your business description and previous jobs to Pollinations for paid text generation. Outputs are drafts, not verified web research. The local 1-Pollen pause is not a hard cap; a call may cross it. Unknown or zero observed debit stops further calls because billing may not have settled. Observed key debit can include concurrent use elsewhere and is not exact call attribution. Approval offers a separately confirmed paid follow-up note only.

            The issued key lives only in this tab's session storage, is never logged or sent anywhere except Pollinations' own API, and is cleared when you close the tab (the issued key remains valid until provider expiry or revocation). Revoke any time from your dashboard.
